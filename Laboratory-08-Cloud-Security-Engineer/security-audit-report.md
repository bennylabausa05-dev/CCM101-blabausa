1. **The Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP): What is it and why is it important for cloud user accounts?**
Users and accounts get only the minimum permissions needed to do their job, and nothing more.
In the cloud, this limits the damage if an account is compromised or misused, since an attacker can only do what that account was allowed to do.

3. **Attack Surface: What does this term mean when applied to a virtual machine?**
It’s the sum of all the ways someone could attack a VM, such as open ports, running services, installed software, user accounts, and exposed interfaces.
The fewer of these there are, the fewer opportunities an attacker has.

5. **Default Deny Policy: Why is it safer to block all traffic by default rather than trying to block specific hackers individually?**
Blocking all traffic by default and allowing only what’s explicitly needed means unknown or new threats are stopped automatically.
Trying to block hackers one by one fails because you can’t know every attacker’s address or method in advance, and they can easily change them.
